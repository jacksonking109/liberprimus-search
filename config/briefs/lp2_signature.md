Sections 10-25 (LP2 pages 17-72), 12,956 runes, Gematria Primus (29 runes). Measured, not guessed:
- Letter frequencies are flat (IC x 29 = 1.00; language 1.7-2.0) and so are the neighbour differences c[t]-c[t-1] (1.03). A simple substitution is out.
- Adjacent equal runes occur at 0.19 of chance (86 against about 440 expected) and only at lag 1; lags 2-8 are at chance. Same in the page images and the text; uniform across runes, pages and sections; not tied to word, line or page position, phase, spacing, neighbouring runes or alphabet class (it looks like random thinning).
- No cipher in this program language reproduces that (58,024 screened: constant, progressive, prime, totient, word-key, Hill, block and feedback forms): either lag 1 stays at 0.35 or more, or the letter or difference statistics stay language-like. An English running key independent of the plaintext gives lag 1 of about 1.0. Repeating keys up to period 60 are excluded.
- A running sum with a keyed multiplicative step, c[t] = c[t-1] + a[t] * n[t] (mod 29), fits the numbers only if the plaintext zero rune is EA, and the repeats' word positions reject EA; no position-keyed multiplier (8 sequences, 3000 offsets) gives a language-like index of coincidence.
- The page images carry no OutGuess data on these pages. A running key that is a phrase (WELCOME PILGRIM TO THE ...) is used elsewhere in the puzzle.
