# Fixture sources

truncated to at most 450000 characters each. Written by `scripts/fetch_corpus.py`.

- `english_melville_moby.txt`: Moby Dick, Herman Melville (Gutenberg #2701), 450000 chars. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GITenberg/Moby-Dick--Or-The-Whale_2701/master/2701.txt
- `english_shelley_frankenstein.txt`: Frankenstein, Mary Shelley (Gutenberg #84), 428986 chars. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GITenberg/Frankenstein_84/master/84.txt
- `english_doyle_adventures.txt`: The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes, Arthur Conan Doyle (Gutenberg #1661), 450000 chars. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GITenberg/The-Adventures-of-Sherlock-Holmes_1661/master/1661.txt
