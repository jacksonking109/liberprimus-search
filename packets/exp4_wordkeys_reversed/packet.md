# Packet exp4_wordkeys_reversed: Repeating word keys under the reversed alphabet, with and without the F-skip

Generated by `python -m lpswarm.research_packets` from development SHA f822be51e2e64c4979562e3cce497626c613f6e9 (Astra research note, 2026-11-05). Approval: **NO: review first, then ask**. Nothing has been run.

## What it tests

Decryption under the reversed alphabet (`map: reverse`, rune i becomes 28 - i, the reversed Gematria the source notes give for solved LP1 pages 1, 5 and 6): `p = (28 - c) - key` or `p = (28 - c) + key`, with `key` a repeating word. Words are the verified keywords (DIVINITY, FIRFUMFERENFE) plus the 5,000 commonest corpus words of three or more letters (the same source as the Phase 1 baseline), deduplicated by encoded key. Each is run twice: with no skip, and with the F-skip resolved per occurrence (`skip F`, `skip_mode resolve`: each ciphertext F may be a literal that does not advance the key, or an encrypted rune). Targets are the five unsolved sections of 308-729 runes, where the period scan has little power; on the 1,000+ rune sections it found no repeating key of period up to about 60 whatever the alphabet orientation, which already argues against this family there.

## Overlap with earlier work

- The Phase 1 baseline ran Vigenere / Beaufort / variant Beaufort with the same word source on every section, but not under `map: reverse` with `sub` or `add`. Beaufort (`p = k - c`) equals the reversed alphabet with the key shifted by one letter, so those key words with that shift are not tested by the baseline either; this packet tests the unshifted words.
- The exhaustive Codex jobs covered every key of length up to 4 with `map: reverse` on sections 11, 19, 21 and 25 (19 never finished, 25 failed). Coverage is unknown until the local ledgers are compared.

## Not in this packet

- Keys that are not words (random keys of length 5 or more), a key phase other than 0, autokey keys, and keys combined with a transposition.
- Constant shifts other than the reversal itself (a constant is absorbed by the key letters, so shifted variants are different words, not different families).
